# Privacy Policy — Patch: Habit Tracker

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

Patch does not collect your data. There is no account, no server and no
analytics, and nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere.

This document exists because Apple requires a privacy policy from any app that
reads Apple Health data, and because a claim like the one above is worth
stating plainly rather than leaving implied.

## What Patch stores

Your goals and everything you log against them, on your device, in the app's
own storage. That is the whole of it.

Patch has no backend. The developer cannot see your goals, cannot see what you
have logged, and has no mechanism by which they could.

## Apple Health

If — and only if — you turn on **Fill in from Apple Health** for a goal, Patch
asks iOS for permission to *read* one of the following, whichever matches that
goal:

- step count
- walking and running distance
- exercise minutes

Patch reads a daily total for the last seven days, uses it to keep that goal up
to date, and stores the result alongside your other entries on your device.

- Patch **never writes** anything to Apple Health.
- Health data is **never** sent off your device, to the developer or to anyone
  else, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.
- You can withdraw permission at any time in **Settings › Privacy & Security ›
  Health › Patch**. Entries already imported stay in Patch, where you can
  remove them like any other entry.

## Notifications

If you turn on daily check-ins, the reminders are scheduled locally by iOS.
They contain no information about your goals or your progress, and no push
server is involved.

## Taking your data out

**Settings › Export** writes your complete data to a JSON or CSV file and hands
it to iOS's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice. Patch does
not upload it.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Patch has stored.

## Children

Patch is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and the
