## Description (4000 max)

Minutes read. Push-ups done. Litres of water. Money put aside when the salary
lands. A day without smoking.

Most habit trackers treat those as different kinds of thing. Patch treats them
as one: an amount you accumulate toward a daily target. That single idea makes
the whole app simpler to use, because every goal works the same way.

TRACK ANYTHING

• Time, counts, distance, volume or money — set a daily target and log against it
• Quick-add buttons sized to the goal, or type an exact amount
• Daily confirmations for the goals that are simply done or not done

A SCHEDULE THAT FITS

• Every day, certain weekdays, or certain dates of the month for things that
  follow a salary
• Days a goal is not scheduled do not break your streak
• Today is forgiving — an unfinished today measures from yesterday rather than
  showing zero

SEE WHERE YOU ARE

• Today: what is left, at a glance
• Calendar: a month at a time, and tap any day to log it — there is no limit on
  how far back you can reach
• Statistics: day, week, month, year or a window you choose, with done against
  missed for every goal

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN

• Small and medium widgets showing today's progress and what is next
• Lock Screen widgets too
• Five page themes that the widget wears as well

APPLE HEALTH, ONLY IF YOU ASK

Turn on Fill in from Apple Health for a goal and Patch keeps it up to date from
steps, distance or exercise minutes. Patch never writes to Health, and reads
nothing unless you turn it on.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS

• No account, no server, no analytics, no ads
• Nothing you enter leaves your iPhone
• Export everything as JSON or a spreadsheet whenever you like

Patch is in English, Ukrainian and Russian.

## App Privacy answers

**Data collection: NO.** Patch collects no data of any kind. There is no
account, no server, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

Health data is read on device with the user's permission and never leaves the
device, which is not "collection" under Apple's definition (it is not
transmitted off the device).

Answer *No* to "Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?"

## App Review notes

No account and no login — the app opens straight into onboarding, which
offers nine goal templates. Pick any and tap Continue.

Apple Health is optional and off by default. To see it: create or edit a
goal measured in time, count or distance, and set "Fill in from Health".
Patch asks only to *read* steps, walking/running distance or exercise
minutes, and never writes. Everything works with Health switched off.

The widget is under the usual Home Screen flow: long-press, Edit, Add
Widget, search for Patch. Small, medium and three Lock Screen sizes.
