# Patch

A goal and habit tracker for iPhone. Reading twenty minutes a day, a hundred
push-ups, two litres of water, money put aside when the salary lands — Patch
treats all of them as the same thing: an amount accumulated toward a daily
target.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?**
On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Patch has no account and no server —
see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Can I get my data out?**
Yes. **Settings › Export** writes everything to a JSON file (a complete backup)
or a CSV (one row per goal per day, for a spreadsheet).

**Does it sync between devices?**
Not yet. Your data transfers with an iPhone backup or when you migrate to a new
iPhone, but two devices do not share a list.

**Can I log a day I forgot?**
Yes. Open **Calendar**, tap the day, tap the goal. There is no limit on how far
back you can reach.

**Why can I not log a goal today?**
It is probably not scheduled for today. **Settings › All Goals** shows every
goal, scheduled or not, and lets you edit, archive or restore any of them.

**What does Apple Health do?**
Nothing unless you ask. Turning on *Fill in from Apple Health* for a goal lets
Patch read one measure — steps, distance or exercise minutes — to keep that goal
up to date. Patch never writes to Health.

## Privacy

[Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) — the short version is that Patch collects
nothing, because there is nowhere for it to go.
